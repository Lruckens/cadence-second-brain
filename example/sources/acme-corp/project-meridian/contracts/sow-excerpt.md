# SOW excerpt — Project Meridian

**Effective date:** 2026-06-30
**Term:** 12 weeks from kickoff, with a 4-week optional extension at the same rate.

Acceptance criteria for phase 1: forecasting model must achieve at least 85% accuracy
against Acme's held-out validation set, measured weekly for two consecutive weeks
before sign-off. Payment milestone 2 (40% of fees) is tied to this acceptance,
not to calendar time — a decision Acme's legal team insisted on after a prior vendor
was paid for late, low-quality work.

Open question: the SOW doesn't specify who owns the validation dataset if the
engagement ends before phase 1 sign-off. Legal has been asked to clarify.
